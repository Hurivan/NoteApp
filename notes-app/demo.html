Scrapbook Editor Demo

Apple Notes-style contenteditable area with drag & drop and paste support

How to Use

  • Type: Click in the editor and type normally
  • Paste Text: Copy and paste any text
  • Paste URLs: Copy a URL and paste it to create a link card
  • Drag Images: Drag image files from your desktop
  • Drag Videos: Drag video files (MP4, MOV, WebM)
  • Drag Files: Drag PDFs or other files to create download links
  • Type Between Cards: Click between cards to keep typing
